## Phase 3C — trend integrity (investigated first, before Phases 1–2 were finished)

14. **Cause of the 17 Oct dip (wheat, 30 days): which mandis reported, not price.** 17 Oct 2026 is a Saturday: 16 mandis reported (normal weekdays 29–33). The same 14 mandis that reported on both 16 and 17 Oct moved by a median of **−₹5**; the daily median of "whoever reported" fell from ₹2,570 to **₹2,526 (−₹44)** only because the mix changed (Sagar ₹2,680 and Malthone reported, 16 others did not; Rehli ₹2,605 → ₹2,210). Details: `evidence/trend-investigation.md`.
15. **The 15 Aug wheat spike has the same cause:** 2 mandis reported that day (median ₹3,263 vs about ₹2,530 around it).
16. **Method: the founder's stable basket, with one stricter number.** Basket = mandis that reported on ≥ 60 % of the window's priced days; daily value = median of the basket mandis that reported; skip a day when too few basket mandis reported; < 3 stable mandis → plain median, labelled "कम मंडियों का भाव". **Deviation:** the brief says "skip when fewer than half of the basket reported"; the data shows half still lets 17 Oct through (13 of 25 basket mandis → still ₹2,526). With **two thirds** it is skipped. Over all crops and windows, ⅔ gives a smaller error against the like-for-like change than ½ (wheat 30 d: worst step error 27 → 20; Urad 30 d: 658 → 452) and drops only a few more days; ¾ drops many more days for little gain (`evidence/trend-threshold-compare.txt`). Alternatives considered: a chain-linked like-for-like index (more accurate, but every point would be a computed level, not a median of real prices that day — conflicts with "every price shown must be a real value"); a fixed-effects adjustment (same problem).
17. **"Window days" = the dates inside the window that have at least one price** (not calendar days). Why: mandis are closed on Sundays/holidays; counting calendar days would empty the basket.
18. **Known limit — Lentil.** For Lentil the basket method's step error is larger than the plain median's (its 7 stable mandis have very different price levels). Kept the one rule for every crop (simpler, explainable); listed in BACKLOG.md.

## Phase 2 — collapsible sections

21. **The arrow goes on section headings (`<h2>`), not on the page title.** The founder's first example, "गेहूं भाव — आज व MSP से तुलना", is the `/msp/gehun` **`<h1>`** (page title), and collapsing the title would hide the whole page. The brief's own spec says the `<h2>` sits in the summary, so every section `<h2>` gets the arrow right after its text ("सभी फसलें — आज का भाव ⌄", "आज का भाव ⌄" …); the `<h1>` stays a plain title. HUMAN-REVIEW.
22. **"First three sections" = the first three collapsible sections** of the page. On the homepage the category tiles are never collapsible (founder), so search, near-you and the listings stay open (the marketplace listings stay visible). Full table: `evidence/collapsible-defaults.md` (HUMAN-REVIEW).
23. **Collapse only below the fold.** After hydration, a secondary section on a phone collapses only if its top is below the screen at that moment, so nothing the visitor sees moves (CLS). A visitor who lands mid-page (scroll restore) keeps that section open.
24. **The chevron is drawn inside the `<h2>`, not a separate `<button>`.** A button inside `<summary>` is nested interactive content (invalid, and it would swallow taps). The whole summary is the button (≥ 44 px, hand cursor, 10E glow on the chevron for a mouse, saffron focus ring, press state); `aria-expanded` is set on the summary, and a screen-reader text "खोलें" / "बंद करें" ("Expand" / "Collapse") follows the heading.
25. **"सभी देखें →" links stay outside the summary**, at the right of the heading row (absolute), so they work and stay visible when the section is collapsed and are never nested in the toggle.
26. **Remembered choice:** one `localStorage` key `ks_sections_v1` (`{"msp:trend":1,…}`), wrapped in try/catch; storage blocked → the toggle still works for the visit. `/msp` and all `/msp/<crop>` pages share the key `msp`. Only real visitor toggles are saved (not defaults, not print).
27. **Print:** `beforeprint` opens every section (set on the element at once, then in state), `afterprint` restores; the chevrons are hidden in print.
28. **The chevron turns in 180 ms** when tapped (feedback to the tap, not ambient animation); no transition at all under reduced motion; no height animation anywhere.
29. **`PageExplainer` and `FaqAccordion` (shared by many pages) got an opt-in `collapsible` prop.** Only `/msp` and `/mausam` pass it; every other page renders exactly as before.
30. **The mandi-compare view got a heading.** It had none; to make it collapsible like the by-crop view it gets an `<h2>` with the existing tab label "मंडी तुलना" (no new wording). It is not in the prerendered HTML (the default view is by crop).
31. **Other pages** (scheme pages, cold storage, kisan mela, sawaal, guide, etc.) are not changed → BACKLOG.
32. **Prerender readiness for `/mausam` and `/yojana` (SEO, visitor-invisible).** Both pages told the prerender "ready" before their data arrived. `/mausam` lost its FAQ block and FAQPage JSON-LD in the Phase 1 and Phase 2 builds (it was there in the Phase 0 build by luck); `/yojana` (and `/yojana/central`, `/yojana/mp`) was captured on its spinner in all three builds (22.6 KB vs 40.0 KB on production). Now `/mausam` is ready once its FAQ request has finished (success or failure) and `/yojana` once its scheme list has loaded. Only the `data-prerender-ready` marker changes. Why: the brief's SEO rule ("nothing lost"), and the ship's compare gate fails pages more than 15 % smaller than live — the 10C production `/mausam` (38.0 KB with FAQ) and `/yojana` (40.0 KB) would fail against loading-state captures. Alternative: leave them (pre-existing; risk of an automatic rollback or a stopped ship).
33. **Ship tooling: compare-gate limits and a pre-deploy size check.** `scripts/verify-batch9-live.mjs` got an opt-in `--min-ratio` (mirror of `--max-growth`; default unchanged). `ship-prod-10f.sh` passes `--min-ratio root=0.70,browse=0.70` (the 10D hero is removed: the staging homepage HTML is 17 % smaller than 10E's) and `--max-growth root=1.40,browse=1.40,msp=1.60` (badge, chart labels, trend table). New step 3b runs the same size rules on the fresh `dist` against the live snapshot BEFORE deploying (`scripts/batch10f/predeploy-size-check.mjs`), re-runs the prerender up to 2 times on a failure, and stops before deploying if it still fails — so a loading-state capture can no longer cause a deploy + automatic rollback.

## Phase 3 — /msp badge, chart dates, trend integrity

34. **Badge colour from the real date, "today" in India (IST).** Green "आज का भाव · 19 अक्टू" when the price date equals today's IST date, otherwise saffron "अंतिम भाव: 17 अक्टू 2026". Badges: next to "आज का भाव" (date of the crop's newest table rows), next to "सभी फसलें — आज का भाव" (newest date among all crops — on staging today that was 19 Oct, green, while wheat's own newest was 17 Oct, saffron) and next to "मंडी तुलना" (newest compared price). The glow pulses 3 times (1.4 s each) then stays as a steady ring; none under reduced motion.
35. **Readable dates on /msp only.** Every visible date on /msp is now "17 अक्टू" / "17 अक्टू 2026" (badge, mandi search result, "डेटा उपलब्ध:", trend table, chart ticks/labels/tooltip, the mandi-compare hint, the "पिछला भाव (…)" tags via an opt-in `fmt` on `PriceCell`/`StaleTag`, and the WhatsApp text). Other pages keep their dd/mm tags (not in scope). JSON-LD keeps ISO dates (machine-readable by design). The WhatsApp text now names the PRICE date, not today's date (it used to put today's date over older prices).
36. **The chart is drawn at the real pixel width** (`src/lib/mandi/chartLayout.js` + `src/components/pages/TrendChart.jsx`; the old `TrendChart` in `shared.jsx` was removed), so text is never scaled down on a phone. x is by calendar date (skipped days show as a bridged gap). Labels are two lines (date / price), placed by a small search (above/below, five horizontal spots, up to two levels further with a thin leader line) so no two labels, the MSP label, the y-axis prices or the date ticks overlap; checked for every crop × 7/30/90 × hi/en × 288/380/1136 px on real staging data (unit) and at 320/412/1280 in the browser (e2e). The left gutter grows for 5-digit prices (garlic ₹12,800).
37. **Touch:** `touch-action: pan-y` on the chart — vertical swipes still scroll the page, a horizontal drag scrubs the dots; the tooltip follows the NEAREST dot (90 dots are too small to hit). Keyboard: the chart is one focusable group (saffron focus ring); ← → Home End move, Esc hides; the current dot is announced in a polite live region.
38. **"सारणी देखें" table** uses the Phase 2 component with two new options (`as="h3"`, `closedByDefault`): closed at every width after hydration (unless it is on screen — CLS rule), open in the prerendered HTML. Newest date first; columns date, price, number of mandis. It replaces the old screen-reader-only table.
39. **Calculator and WhatsApp use the corrected series** of the selected window: the price is the window's last plotted point, with its date and number of mandis shown ("हिसाब का भाव: ₹2,560 (16 अक्टू, 24 मंडियों का बीच का भाव)"); today's plain median is only a fallback when there is no series. The by-mandi table above still shows each mandi's real latest price.
40. **Skipped days are said out loud** under the chart ("10 दिन बहुत कम मंडियों के भाव आए — …"), next to the existing "N दिन का भाव उपलब्ध" note; the tab ranges and the trend sentence use the plotted days only. As a result the chart can end a day before the newest table date (wheat: chart 16 Oct, table 17 Oct) — by design, HUMAN-REVIEW.
41. **Old tests updated (test-only):** `msp-trend-10e` reads the new dot/tick hooks; `phase19` accepts "(17 अक्टू)" as well as "(17/10)"; `collapsible-10f` C3 ignores the trend table (closed by design).
