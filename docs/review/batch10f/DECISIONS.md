## Phase 3C — trend integrity (investigated first, before Phases 1–2 were finished)

14. **Cause of the 17 Oct dip (wheat, 30 days): which mandis reported, not price.** 17 Oct 2026 is a Saturday: 16 mandis reported (normal weekdays 29–33). The same 14 mandis that reported on both 16 and 17 Oct moved by a median of **−₹5**; the daily median of "whoever reported" fell from ₹2,570 to **₹2,526 (−₹44)** only because the mix changed (Sagar ₹2,680 and Malthone reported, 16 others did not; Rehli ₹2,605 → ₹2,210). Details: `evidence/trend-investigation.md`.
15. **The 15 Aug wheat spike has the same cause:** 2 mandis reported that day (median ₹3,263 vs about ₹2,530 around it).
16. **Method: the founder's stable basket, with one stricter number.** Basket = mandis that reported on ≥ 60 % of the window's priced days; daily value = median of the basket mandis that reported; skip a day when too few basket mandis reported; < 3 stable mandis → plain median, labelled "कम मंडियों का भाव". **Deviation:** the brief says "skip when fewer than half of the basket reported"; the data shows half still lets 17 Oct through (13 of 25 basket mandis → still ₹2,526). With **two thirds** it is skipped. Over all crops and windows, ⅔ gives a smaller error against the like-for-like change than ½ (wheat 30 d: worst step error 27 → 20; Urad 30 d: 658 → 452) and drops only a few more days; ¾ drops many more days for little gain (`evidence/trend-threshold-compare.txt`). Alternatives considered: a chain-linked like-for-like index (more accurate, but every point would be a computed level, not a median of real prices that day — conflicts with "every price shown must be a real value"); a fixed-effects adjustment (same problem).
17. **"Window days" = the dates inside the window that have at least one price** (not calendar days). Why: mandis are closed on Sundays/holidays; counting calendar days would empty the basket.
18. **Known limit — Lentil.** For Lentil the basket method's step error is larger than the plain median's (its 7 stable mandis have very different price levels). Kept the one rule for every crop (simpler, explainable); listed in BACKLOG.md.

Cause: the LOCAL preview server answers `/guide` and `/about` (no trailing slash) with the prerendered **homepage** HTML (`curl` check in `evidence/phase1/guide-f1-recheck.txt` context: `/guide` → `home-hero` present, `/guide/` → `guide-hub`), whose lazy listing photos start loading before the app swaps in the guide page. The new hero is ~940 px shorter, so one more photo is inside the browser's lazy-load distance. Not a guide request, not a staging behaviour (Cloudflare serves `guide/index.html`). Fix: the comparison ignores plain public storage image GETs; the real guard ("no request started by guide code", `fromGuide`) is unchanged. After: F1 8/8, three runs in a row. Alternative: a homepage change to delay listing photos (product change for a test artifact; rejected).
