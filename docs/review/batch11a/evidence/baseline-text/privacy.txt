If you share your device location, or type or pick your village or town, we use it only to show offers near you, mandi prices, weather, and how far melas, cold storages and search results are from you. It is saved in your browser on this device, not in your account or profile. If you are logged in and no place is saved on this device, the village location already in your profile is used. To find offers near you, our database is asked for offers in the area around you; this is not saved to your account. To show the weather, your position is first rounded to an area of about 10 km. Your exact position is not saved for weather. To name your place, the position may be sent to the map service BigDataCloud, and a place name you ask us to look up on the map goes to OpenStreetMap. You can change it anytime with the "Change" link next to your place name.
