40. **3B phone rule (`hasPhoneNumber`, `src/lib/listings/extraInfo.js`).** Blocked: any run of 10+ digits where only spaces, tabs, dashes (incl. en dash), dots, brackets or "+" stand between digits: "9876543210", "98765 43210", "+91-98765-43210", "(+91) 98765.43210", "9 8 7 6 5 4 3 2 1 0", and Hindi digits (read as 0-9). Allowed: 9 digits; Indian prices with commas ("₹1,20,000", "1,20,00,000"; commas break a run); years ("2024 का मॉडल"); "1200-1500"; dates with a 4-digit year ("15-10-2026 - 20-10-2026"; dates are taken out first); numbers on separate lines. Known false positive (accepted, safest side): several plain numbers joined only by spaces/dashes that add up to 10+ digits, e.g. "2023 2024 2025". Why: the login wall on phone numbers is a fraud rule; erring on blocking costs the farmer one edit. Alternative: only 10-digit numbers starting 6-9 (misses split "+91 ..." numbers and other tricks).
