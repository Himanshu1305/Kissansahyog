# How much reviewed content the post needs - Batch 21

For 90 days (about 13 weeks) the same weekday should not repeat an item. Below: what that takes, against what exists today. "Reviewed" is what can go out live; "seeded" is what is in the files waiting for a person. Numbers are from `npm run morning:content` (9 Nov 2026).

| Day / slot | Needed for 90 days | Seeded (not reviewed) | Reviewed today | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon scheme news + Fri how-to | about **26** (13 schemes, each with a news line and a how-to) | 5 schemes for Monday (pm-kisan-24, pmfby-rabi, and the 3 older ones); **0 usable how-tos** (the only one, pmfby-rabi, has no last date) | 0 | 26 |
| Tue "अब आगे क्या?" | **13** dated things (fairs, festivals, deadlines, windows) | 9 events that can show (13 stored, 4 far-away ones are hidden) | 0 | 4 more, and refreshed as dates pass |
| Wed money fact | **13** | 1 (the MSP news; shown once a month only, because a pool under 4 is throttled) | 0 | 12 |
| Thu "क्या आप जानते हैं?" | **13** | 13 (12 general lines with no source, 1 sourced MSP line) | 0 | 13 sourced ones |
| Sat kahawat | **13** | 12 | 0 | 1, plus Shri A.K. Dixit's check of all |
| Task line (every day) | a wheat calendar line for each date window, 1 Nov 2026 - 18 Feb 2027 | 10 stage lines + 1 rain line (1 line too long) | 0 | KVK Sagar confirmation; then gram, mustard, lentil |

**Rule of thumb:** until a weekday has 4 reviewed items, each item shows at most once in 4 weeks and the other weeks fall back to "अब आगे क्या?" or no slot. So the first 4 reviewed items per weekday matter most.
