crop × problem and each scheme, (b) the actual on-page entities, and (c) the Kisan
Sawaal demand research (`docs/research/QA_DEMAND.md`). Where a keyword came from
autocomplete/PAA it is marked (AC)/(PAA). Used naturally in title, H1, first 100
words, H2s and FAQ — no stuffing.
