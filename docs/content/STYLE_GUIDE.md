# Kissan Sahyog — Content Style Guide (Batch 3)

Who reads us: farmers, vendors and machine owners around **Sagar (Bundelkhand, MP)**,
mostly on phones. Officials also read the carbon page. Write for them, not for an
editor. Words only — no layout or feature changes in this batch.

The rule behind every rule: **a farmer should be able to act after reading.**
If a paragraph tells the reader nothing they can do, decide, call or carry — cut it.

---

## 1. Hindi — write it first, never translate it

- Write the way an educated person from Sagar **talks to** a farmer: simple everyday
  Hindi, with the English words people actually use — **ट्रैक्टर, मशीन, ऑनलाइन,
  पोर्टल, सब्सिडी, लोन, मोबाइल, फ़ॉर्म, लिस्ट, वेबसाइट, फ़ोटो**.
- Address the reader as **आप**.
- **Active voice. Short sentences — aim for 15 words or fewer, never more than 22.**
  One idea per sentence. Break long sentences at "और", "लेकिन", "ताकि", ",".
- Each paragraph must answer a real question a farmer would ask:
  **यह क्या है · कितना मिलेगा · कहाँ आवेदन करूँ · कौन से कागज़ · क्या गड़बड़ हो सकती है · किसे फ़ोन करूँ.**
- Prefer **lists, small tables and numbered steps** over long paragraphs.
- Numbers in **Indian format**: ₹1,50,000 — not ₹150,000.
- Numbers, dates, ₹ amounts, scheme rules, chemical names/doses, laws and rulings
  are **facts** — they come only from the cited source and must not change. Reword
  the sentence around a fact; never the fact.

## 2. English — written separately, from the same facts

- Plain, short and direct. Not a word-for-word translation of the Hindi.
- Same facts, same numbers, same cites. Short sentences. Active voice.
- A Sagar farmer who reads English, or an official, should find it clear and fast.

---

## 3. The संक्षेप में short answer (Q&As)

Every Q&A gets a **संक्षेप में** short answer of **at most 50 words**, in Hindi and
English. It is the one-breath answer: what to do, in plain words, no padding.

---

## 4. Legacy Q&A structure

Rewrite each legacy answer into:
1. **Short answer** (संक्षेप में).
2. **पहचान / Signs** — how to identify.
3. **क्या करें / What to do, in order:** cultural → biological → chemical.
4. **रोकथाम / Prevention.**
5. **KVK सागर को कब फ़ोन करें / When to call KVK Sagar.**

Name chemicals and doses **only exactly as the cited source gives them**, always
with **"लेबल पर लिखी मात्रा ही उपयोग करें"**. A fact you cannot source is removed;
a whole answer you cannot source means the row is unpublished and listed in the report.

---

## 5. The read-aloud test

Before committing any page, read 5 random paragraphs aloud in your head. If any
sounds like a **government circular** or a **translation**, rewrite it.

Tell-tale signs it failed: a sentence over 22 words; a chain of "के द्वारा / के
माध्यम से / के अंतर्गत"; a paragraph that states a fact but no action; passive voice
("की जाती है" where "करें/मिलता है" works).
