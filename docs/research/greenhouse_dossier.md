## B. DETAILED SECTIONS

### 1. Basics

**1.1 Definition and how it works**
- "Protected cultivation is a process of growing crops in a controlled environment." — NCERT vocational text (kepc101), https://ncert.nic.in/vocational/pdf/kepc101.pdf [Official]
- How it works (NCERT): "When short wave radiation from the Sun enters the greenhouse structure, it … gets transformed into long wave radiations. These long wave radiations do not escape the greenhouse in entirety, thereby trapping the heat." [Official]
- Practices listed by NCERT: "forced ventilated greenhouse, naturally ventilated polyhouse, insect-proof net house, shade net house, plastic tunnel and mulching, raised beds, trellising and drip irrigation." [Official]
- Soil for greenhouse (NCERT): "pH of 6.0–6.5 and electrical conductivity should be less than 0.5 dS/m"; water need "approximately 1–2 l/m²/day". [Official]

**1.2 Structure types** (sources: NCERT; ICAR *Indian Horticulture* 2023; NHB and MIDH cost norms)

| Type | What it is | Verified notes |
|---|---|---|
| Naturally ventilated polyhouse (NVPH) | GI-pipe frame with UV film; side and roof vents, no fans | NHB norm ₹844/m² (tubular). MIDH slabs ₹1,060 to ₹844 by area. Haryana spec: ridge 6.5–7 m, gutter 4–4.5 m, top vent 80–90 cm with 40-mesh screen [Official] |
| Fan-and-pad / climate-controlled greenhouse | Exhaust fans plus wet cooling pads | NHB ₹1,400/m²; MIDH slabs ₹1,650 to ₹1,400 [Official] |
| Shade net house | GI frame covered with shade net (e.g. 50%) | NHB tubular ₹710/m²; flowers not allowed under NHB. ICAR: leafy vegetables "can successfully be raised during April to July with 40-60% shading intensity" [Official] |
| Insect-proof net house | 40-mesh nylon net (IS 16513:2016) | Haryana: ₹525/m² (1,008–2,080 m²), ₹500 (2,080–4,000 m²) [Official, reference] |
| Walk-in tunnel | Tunnel 2–3 m high | NHB ₹600/m². ICAR: "suitable for all types of vegetable crops like okra, brinjal, tomato, chilli" [Official] |
| Low tunnel / plastic tunnel | Low hoops with film | NHB ₹60/m². Haryana ₹29/m² (plastic) or ₹25 (non-woven). ICAR: low-tunnel "film of 100 µ is sufficient" [Official] |
| Plastic mulching | Film laid on the beds | NHB ₹32,000/ha; MP press ₹0.32 lakh/ha, up to 2 ha [Official]/[Press] |

**1.3 Crops for MP and Bundelkhand (verified only)**
- Crops grown under protected structures in MP, per ICAR PFDC Bhopal: "onion, garlic, potato, tomato, chilli, capsicum, cucumber, broccoli" and flowers "Gerbera, Roses". https://icar.org.in/en/node/4630 [Official]
- NCERT's main protected crops: rose, gerbera, carnation, chrysanthemum, lilium, orchid; tomato, capsicum, cucumber, leafy vegetables. [Official]
- Nursery raising: MIDH 2025 refers to a "Hi-tech green-house having insect proof netting on sides and fogging and misting systems (only for plug type hi-tech nursery)". [Official]
- No crop study specific to Sagar or Bundelkhand was found.

**1.4 Benefits (verified figures)**
- Yield 3–10× open field (ICAR IIVR, *Indian Horticulture* 2023). [Official]
- Yields per plant under NV polyhouse (same article): two-stem tomato 11.0 kg/plant, cherry tomato 2.3 kg/plant, capsicum 2.5 kg/plant. [Official]
- Drip and micro-irrigation: water saving 20–48%, fertiliser saving 11–19%, yield +20–38% (PIB, 29 Aug 2026, citing Economic Survey 2020-21). [Official]
- Off-season production: PIB (Aug 2025 horticulture backgrounder) — "production of high-value vegetables and flowers, including off-season varieties." [Official]
- Employment: "four to five rural labourers" per structure in MP (ICAR PFDC Bhopal). [Official]
- Labour: "four labourers are required for flower cultivation in a one-acre greenhouse" (NCERT). [Official]

### 2. Step-by-step setup (verified elements; Haryana rules used as best-practice reference where MP's rules are not accessible)

1. **Plan and choose a crop and market.** No source given; this is advisory.
2. **Site and soil/water test.** Haryana: "Soil and water test report For site suitability Mandatory". NCERT soil target pH 6.0–6.5, EC <0.5 dS/m. [Official, reference]
3. **Training.** Haryana: a "minimum three days training-cum-workshop" is mandatory. [Official, reference] For MP, ICAR PFDC Bhopal trained over 100 farmers (2012-18). [Official]
4. **Register on MPFSTS and apply** (MP): portal https://mpfsts.mp.gov.in/mphd/#/ ; free; landholders; Aadhaar link (CM Helpline). Documents (Krishak Jagat, 17 Jun 2026): "आधार कार्ड, बैंक पासबुक, ऋण पुस्तिका अथवा खसरा नकल", photo, mobile, caste certificate for SC/ST. [Official]/[Press]
5. **Selection by lottery when demand exceeds target.** "लक्ष्य से अधिक आवेदनों को … लॉटरी के माध्यम से चयन किया जायेगा" (Krishak Jagat, 31 Jan 2023, about MPFSTS). [Press] The 2020 MP notice lists conditions: private agricultural land, adequate irrigation, own funds or a bank loan (Kisan Samadhan, Aug 2020). [Press]
6. **Design and structure specs** (BIS; Haryana spec as reference): film "UV stabilized 200 micron … (IS 15827: 2019)" with at least 85% light transmission, anti-drip and anti-fog; insect net 40-mesh (IS 16513:2016); shade net (IS 16008:2016); GI pipe IS 1239, galvanising IS 4736; NVPH column 76 mm OD, 2 mm wall. MIDH 2025: "All material/technologies should conform to BIS standards" (reported in section 7.25). [Official]
7. **Drip and fertigation.** MIDH 2025 says protected cultivation must be integrated with drip, or converged with another scheme where MIDH does not fund the drip. Drip subsidy via PMKSY is 55%/45%. MP RKVY pilot for "sensor-based automation systems for fertigation" gives 50% subsidy, chosen by lottery (Krishak Jagat, 23 Aug 2025, Dewas). [Official]/[Press]
8. **Build, inspect, release subsidy.** Haryana flow (reference): online application → document check → site verification → sanction → build within 90 days → first inspection (material and foundation) → final inspection → physical verification → release. Third-party inspection with GPS coordinates; insurance mandatory. NHB route: online IPA, bank term-loan sanction, physical file within 30 days, Joint Inspection Team (bank + NHB + state), geo-tagged photos with officer and promoter in frame, notarised affidavit on ₹20 stamp paper. [Official]
9. **Keep and maintain.** Haryana: structure must be used "for a minimum of 5 years … failing which the beneficiary is bound to return the assistance". Cladding replacement aid "on 3 years old green house" (70% aid in Haryana: polyhouse cladding ₹153/m²). [Official, reference]
   - Film life: no official source found that states a life in years. Only Haryana's "3 years old" eligibility rule was found. See Not-verified.

**3.3 Sample calculations [Calc]**

These assume the slab rate applies to the whole area, which is how MP press presents it.
- 4,000 m² NVPH × ₹844 = ₹33.76 lakh norm cost; 50% = **₹16.88 lakh** subsidy.
  - This matches exactly the ₹16,88,000 subsidy reported for Rakesh Patidar's 4,000 m² polyhouse in Khargone (Kisan Tak, 15 Nov 2024). That suggests MP used the ₹844 slab in 2024.
- 1,000 m² NVPH × ₹935 = ₹9.35 lakh; 50% = ₹4.675 lakh.
- 4,000 m² shade net × ₹710 = ₹28.40 lakh; 50% = ₹14.20 lakh.
- 2,080–4,000 m² fan-pad × ₹1,400 → 4,000 m² = ₹56 lakh; 50% = ₹28 lakh.
- Actual vendor quotes are usually above the norm. The subsidy is on the lower of the norm and the actual cost. Haryana states this explicitly: the lower of "departmental rates or negotiated rates" [Official, reference].

### 7. Risks and frauds
- **Khargone (MP) polyhouse loan fraud, Sep 2026** [Press: Free Press Journal 23 Sep 2026; INH News 24 Sep 2026]
  - EOW booked 11 people, including former branch manager K.K. Sarvate, loan officer Shubham Sohni, officer Chandrashekar Rathod (Bank of Baroda, per INH), and four private firms.
  - Loans of ₹7,80,85,000 were sanctioned in the names of 33 farmers under the "National Horticulture Mission" polyhouse programme, plus a ₹1.90 cr loan after a land-use change on mortgaged land. Total alleged loss ₹9.70 cr.
  - Firms and allotments: Kisan Agro 20 polyhouses (0 built); Siran Techno 8 (0 built); AVI/Agriculture Solutions 5 (3 built).
  - Detection: "three instances where the work completion certificate submitted was dated the same day the loan amount was disbursed".
  - Method: farmers' land documents were taken, loans paid directly to firms, and fake completion certificates prepared.
- **Built-in safeguards to explain to farmers (verified):**
  - NHB: JIT inspection; geo-tagged photos showing the bank officer and promoter; affidavit; claims accepted only by speed post or authorised messenger "to eliminate … fake subsidy claims"; subsidy refundable if the loan turns NPA.
  - Haryana (reference): third-party inspection with GPS; insurance mandatory; 5-year use condition; option to lab-test materials.
- **Farmer protection checklist (advisory, built from the verified facts above):**
  1. Apply only yourself on MPFSTS. Keep your own login and OTP; never hand over your Aadhaar OTP.
  2. Never sign blank papers or loan documents for a vendor.
  3. Insist that loan disbursal follows work stages and inspection. Watch for a completion certificate dated the same day as disbursal.
  4. Get the vendor's GST, written quotation, BIS marks on film and nets, a material bill, and a warranty in writing.
  5. Check film thickness (200 micron) and IS 15827 marking; take geo-tagged photos at each stage.
  6. Check your bank loan account statement every month.
  7. Report problems to the district horticulture office, CM Helpline 181, or the MPFSTS helpline 0755-4059242 (press-reported).
