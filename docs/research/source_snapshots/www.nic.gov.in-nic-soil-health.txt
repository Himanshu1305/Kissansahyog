It facilitates generation of Soil Health Cards (SHC) in 22 different languages, 5 dialects and in local units for the beneﬁt of farmers in uniform and standardized format across the country. A SHC provides the farmer with the nutrient status of his land and gives recommendations on the dosage of fertilizers, bio-fertilizers, organic fertilizers as well as soil amendments to maintain soil health in the long run.
