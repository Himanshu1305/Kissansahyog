ENFORCEMENT ACTIVITIES
The BIS Standard Mark (ISI Mark) is a quality mark and also has established its brand image for more than six decades as the consumer is always inclined towards quality products.
Genuine ISI Mark
LICENCE NUMBER Licence number (a seven digit number, represented as CM/L --------) is also given along with the Standard Mark. This helps in identifying the unit which has manufactured the product at a specific location.
WHAT DOES IT CONVEY? Conveys that the product carrying the ImageMark conforms to the relevant Indian Standard certified by BIS.
Spurious ISI Mark
If you encounter any below ISI marking on the product purchased:
Like As per IS....... Fitted with ISI...... As per ISS OR COLOURABLE IMITATION OF ISI MONOGRAM
If you found above marking on the product, you are purchasing a fake ISI marked product, please inform us at Dy Director General (Consumer Affairs).
