New 7 Mandis integration in Rajasthan. 7 new mandis added on eNAM in .. News Archive Home >> --> Home     Traders How do I register? Registration of Buyers/ Trader in e-NAM can be done through following ways viz. - Through e-NAM Portal- http://www.enam.gov.in - Through Mobile Application - Through Mandi Registration (by physically visiting a mandi) What are the fees and document required? There is no fee for registration on e-NAM. Required Documents and details for registration : - Basic mandatory details like Name, Sex, Address, DOB, Mobile Number, Bank details etc. - Required doucuments : Passbook (Cancelled cheque), Any Govt ID Card, trading license and other concerned documents. What are the benefits for traders? Benefits from e-NAM for Buyers/ Traders are as follows: - Extended reach to other mandis, hence access to more sellers - Access to a larger and integrated markets - Access to real time information on arrival, quality and price of commodities - Ease of doing business through Mobile-App - Access to online banking and payments How can I get a better quality produce? e-NAM facilitates quality assaying to 219 commodities based on DMI specified pararmeters. Quality of the produce can be assessed through assaying certificate provided by the authorities that can be viewed on the mobile app also. How should I pay the seller? e-NAM encourages ePayments and it can be made through various facilities viz. RTGS, NEFT, Debit/ Credit Card, BHIM. What are the methods of trading?
