### A. Punjab and other states

#### A1. Punjab Forest Department agroforestry carbon credit scheme (TERI)
- **Launch:** On 6 August 2024 (a Tuesday), at the Van Mahotsav in Hoshiarpur, by CM Bhagwant Mann. [Press]
  - Quote: "3686 farmers from across the state will be disbursed Rs 45 crore in four installments"
  - The first instalment was Rs 1.75 crore, paid to 818 farmers in Hoshiarpur district. The implementation partner is TERI.
  - Quote: "Punjab was the first state in the country to launch the carbon credit scheme"
  - Quote: "it is based on the 'Polluter Pays Principle' where polluters compensate the organisations and farmers working to reduce carbon emissions"
  - URL: https://aninews.in/news/national/general-news/punjab-cm-mann-exhorts-people-to-transform-plantation-drives-into-mass-movement-launches-carbon-credit-scheme-worth-rs-45-crore20240806201342/ (ANI, 6 Aug 2024)
- **Districts:** Hoshiarpur, Ropar, Mohali, Pathankot and Nawanshahr. [Third-party]
  - Quote: "Rs 1.75 crore has already been disbursed to 818 farmers in Hoshiarpur"
  - The scheme runs on a five-year stewardship period. Partners are the Forest Department, TERI and "an international consortium".
  - URL: https://www.panj.org.in/aligning-punjab-with-the-emerging-logic-of-climate-markets/ (PANJ Foundation, 31 May 2025)
- **Supporting detail from Changemakr Asia (25 Oct 2024):** [Press/Third-party]
  - The first-instalment compensation went to farmers from the Kandi region.
  - One farmer planted eucalyptus and poplar on a 17-acre farm.
  - Trees take 5–6 years before harvest.
  - Quote from Dr Sanjeev Tiwari, Forest Conservator: "This initiative creates a sustainable model that benefits both the ecosystem and the farming community"
  - URL: https://changemakr.asia/punjab-farmers-reap-rs-1-75-crore-in-carbon-credit-compensation-pioneering-sustainable-agro-forestry/
- **Verra project:** [Third-party aggregator; not confirmed on the Verra registry itself, which would not render in my fetch]
  - climateprojectsregistry.org lists "Punjab Agroforestry", Verra (VCS) ID 2552, with these details:
    - Proponent: Value Network Ventures
    - Area: 3,992.71 ha in SAS Nagar, Rupnagar and Nawanshahr
    - Species: clonal poplar, clonal eucalyptus, teak, shisham, chandan and drek, planted 2017–2020
    - Validation/verification body: SGS UK
    - "Carbon Credits Retired: 950 to date"
  - The REDD projects database (Idrecco) also lists a sister project, "Improving rural livelihood through agroforestry practices in Punjab, India – II" (Hoshiarpur, Dasuya and Pathankot; 3,309.56 ha). Its credit totals look implausible (tens of millions), so **do not publish credit totals**.
  - URLs: https://climateprojectsregistry.org/data-dashboard/6a459296-2537-47e1-b2ce-2e7a330c4c53 ; https://www.reddprojectsdatabase.org/702-improving-rural-livelihood-through-agroforestry-practices-in-punjab-india-ii/ ; Verra comment page: https://verra.org/projects_public_comm/improving-rural-livelihood-through-agroforestry-practices-in-punjab-india-ii/
- **Instalments paid after the first one:** No source found. See the "Not verified" list.
- **Benefit-sharing percentage:** No primary source found. The aamaadmiparty.wiki page (a party-affiliated wiki, so not neutral) says payments are calculated from Verified Emission Reductions measured by TERI. Do not publish a percentage.
- **Calculation:**
  - ₹1.75 crore ÷ 818 = **≈ ₹21,400 per farmer** (first instalment, average)
  - ₹45 crore ÷ 3,686 = **≈ ₹1.22 lakh per farmer** across all 4 instalments, *if paid in full*. This is a promise, not a payout.

#### A3. Was carbon trading "recently allowed" in Punjab? Fact-check
- **No Punjab cabinet decision or notification from 2025–26 that "allows" carbon trading was found.**
  - I checked Punjab-specific searches (cabinet, DIPR, notification) in English and Hindi. Results showed only the 2024 Forest Department scheme, the September 2026 Grow Indigo payments, and unrelated cabinet items.
  - Absence of evidence is not proof. Ask the founders who told them and where. Before publishing, also do a final check on https://diprpunjab.gov.in.
- **Why states don't "allow" it:** Carbon credit trading is set up at national level.
  - The Carbon Credit Trading Scheme, 2023 was notified by the Government of India in June 2023 and amended in December 2023 to add the Offset Mechanism. [Official] PIB, 28 Mar 2025.
  - The voluntary market (Verra and similar) works through private registries. Neither needs a state "permission".
- **What is true, in order:**
  1. **Aug 2024:** Punjab's Forest Department launched an agroforestry carbon-credit compensation scheme (₹45 cr; 3,686 farmers; ₹1.75 cr paid to 818).
  2. **Jul 2025:** A Supreme Court–appointed committee suggested carbon-credit pilots in Kandi (Punjab) and Yamunanagar (Haryana).
  3. **Sep 2026:** A private developer (Grow Indigo) paid ₹2.9 cr to about 2,550 farmers in Punjab and Haryana, at an ICAR-led event at PAU Ludhiana. This was most likely the trigger for the rumour.
- **Possible sources of confusion:**
  - **(a)** Pakistan's Punjab has a "CM Punjab Green Credit Program". The pages describing it are unofficial, *not verified* (ncf.org.pk, pakalerts). The Pakistani Punjab P&D department also held a 3 Nov 2023 meeting titled "Exploring Avenues for Securing Carbon Credit Financing in Punjab" (https://pnd.punjab.gov.pk/node/2684). [Official, Pakistan]
  - **(b)** Indian Punjab's PPCB signed an emissions-trading pact in 2021 for **particulate** pollution from 200 dyeing units in Ludhiana. It is not about farmers.
    - Quote: "Punjab is now becoming the second Indian state to adopt this pioneering vision."
    - URL: https://epic.uchicago.edu/news/punjab-inks-pact-to-launch-emissions-trading/ (EPIC, 7 Jun 2021)
- **Verdict for the page:** "भ्रामक / आंशिक रूप से सही" (misleading / partly true).

### B. National framework

- **CCTS 2023 and the offset mechanism.** [Official] PIB, Ministry of Power, 28 Mar 2025.
  - Quote: "Government of India has already notified The Carbon Credit Trading Scheme, 2023 in June 2023… In December 2023, the scheme was amended to introduce the Offset Mechanism, enabling participation from non-obligated entities through voluntary climate mitigation projects."
  - The release approved the "Detailed Procedure for the Offset Mechanism" and eight methodologies, which it describes as covering "renewable energy (including Hydro and Pumped storage), green hydrogen production, industrial energy efficiency, landfill methane recovery, and mangrove afforestation & reforestation."
  - **The PIB release does not list all 8 by exact name.** BEE's methodology page could not be read, and the proxy blocked beeindia.gov.in. The exact titles of the 8 are listed as Not verified.
  - URL: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2116421
- **Farm methodologies under the offset mechanism.** [Press] (PTI report of a Lok Sabha written reply by MoS Agriculture Ramnath Thakur, 21 Jul 2026)
  - The two approved methodologies, verbatim: "Methane Recovery from Livestock and Manure Management at Households and Small Farms" and "Emission Reduction through Improved Management Practices in Rice Cultivation"
  - Quote: "The Agriculture Ministry is supporting 11 pilot projects on the voluntary carbon market for credit generation in the sector"
  - The pilots are run by 7 ICAR institutes and 4 state agricultural universities. Neither source names them.
  - Quote: "The PoA framework seeks to bring small and marginal farmers into the fold by allowing Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs), NGOs and project developers to aggregate multiple small-scale farm-level activities under a common umbrella for carbon credit generation"

### FAQ (Hindi, with answer notes)
1. **कार्बन क्रेडिट क्या होता है?** (What is a carbon credit?) One credit = 1 tonne CO₂e reduced or removed, verified, and sold to companies.
2. **क्या किसान कार्बन क्रेडिट से पैसा कमा सकते हैं?** (Can farmers earn from carbon credits?) Yes, in some projects: in Sep 2026, 2,550 farmers in Punjab and Haryana got ₹2.9 cr. But amounts are small and late.
3. **पंजाब के किसानों को कितना पैसा मिला?** (How much did Punjab farmers get?) Typically ₹3,000–15,000. The company reports an average of ₹11,478.
4. **क्या पंजाब सरकार ने कार्बन ट्रेडिंग की अनुमति दी?** (Did the Punjab government allow carbon trading?) No such 2025–26 decision was found. The scheme is national. The Forest Department ran a scheme in Aug 2024.
5. **पंजाब वन विभाग की कार्बन क्रेडिट योजना क्या है?** (What is the Punjab Forest Department scheme?) ₹45 cr for 3,686 farmers in 4 instalments. The first was ₹1.75 cr to 818 farmers.
6. **एक एकड़ से कितना कार्बन पैसा मिलता है?** (How much per acre?) There is no fixed rate. Aadi works out to about ₹320 per acre per year (a calculation).
7. **पैसा कितने समय में मिलता है?** (How long until payment?) Aadi: practices from 2019–22, paid in 2026. In UP, payments come every 5 years.
8. **किसान को कितना हिस्सा मिलता है?** (What share does the farmer get?) Aadi 75% (or an upfront payment); Ragini up to 60%; Jharkhand 80%. Always check the contract.
9. **क्या मध्य प्रदेश में कोई कार्बन क्रेडिट योजना है?** (Does MP have a scheme?) No state scheme for farmers. Private projects: Ragini (MP among 4 states) and Varaha in Khargone.
10. **ग्रीन क्रेडिट और कार्बन क्रेडिट में क्या फर्क है?** (Green credit vs carbon credit?) GCP is degraded forest land funded by companies. Its credits are not tradable (Aug 2025). It is not farmer income.
11. **कौन सी खेती की प्रथाएं कार्बन क्रेडिट देती हैं?** (Which practices earn credits?) DSR, zero/reduced tillage, residue management, AWD, agroforestry, rock dust (ERW).
12. **भारत में कार्बन क्रेडिट का ट्रेडिंग कब शुरू होगा?** (When does trading start in India?) Expected around Oct 2026 on power exchanges. Re-check the status.
13. **धान की खेती से कार्बन क्रेडिट मिल सकता है?** (Can rice farming earn credits?) Yes. "Improved Management Practices in Rice Cultivation" is an approved methodology (Lok Sabha reply, Jul 2026).
14. **पशुपालक/गोबर गैस से कार्बन क्रेडिट?** (Livestock or biogas?) Yes. A methodology exists: "Methane Recovery from Livestock and Manure Management at Households and Small Farms".
15. **क्या सब किसानों को पैसा मिला है?** (Has every farmer been paid?) No. A study found >99% of participating farmers in Haryana and MP had received nothing at the time.
16. **कार्बन क्रेडिट कंपनी असली है या फर्जी, कैसे पहचानें?** (How to spot a fake company?) Project ID, registry, written Hindi contract, no upfront fee. Use the checklist.
17. **क्या खेत की मेड़ पर पेड़ लगाकर कमाई हो सकती है?** (Can I earn from trees on field boundaries?) UP: "Rs 250 to Rs 350 per tree" in the TERI scheme. Plus timber value.
18. **MP में नीलगिरी/पोपलर काटने-ले जाने के लिए परमिट चाहिए?** (Do I need a permit for eucalyptus/poplar in MP?) As of Jan 2026, no transit permit is needed for 5 species on private land, but felling needs revenue-department permission.
19. **सरकार ने किसानों की कार्बन आय का आकलन किया है?** (Has the government assessed farmers' carbon income?) No. The July 2026 Lok Sabha reply says "no assessment".
20. **किसान सहयोग पर क्या मिलेगा?** (What will Kisan Sahyog offer?) Information, discussion, and the suggestion box. No transactions or promises.
