## Full description (max 4000)

<!-- FIELD:full -->
Kissan Sahyog is a simple, free app for farmers. Useful farming information and a local farm marketplace in one place, in Hindi.

The app is built for farmers of Sagar Division in Madhya Pradesh (Sagar, Damoh, Panna, Tikamgarh, Chhatarpur, Niwari).

What you get:

• Mandi prices — crop prices from the mandis of Sagar Division, always shown with their date. Prices come from the government source Agmarknet / data.gov.in.
• MSP — the Minimum Support Price of crops, compared with mandi prices.
• Weather — the weather for your area and a forecast for the coming days (this is not an official IMD warning).
• Government schemes — farmer schemes of the Central and Madhya Pradesh governments in simple words, with a link to the official source.
• Kisan Sawaal — ask a farming question and read answers.
• Cold storage — a list of cold storages in Madhya Pradesh, by district.
• Kisan Mela — information about upcoming farm fairs.
• Farm marketplace — tractors and farm equipment on rent, farm workers, Drone Didi, bhusa (fodder), farm inputs such as seed and fertiliser, warehouses and cold storage, greenhouses, transport, and land on lease — post your own listing or see listings near you.
• Voice search — tap the mic and speak in Hindi.
• Works without internet — the mandi prices, weather and other public information you last opened are still shown when there is no network, together with the time they are from.
• Hindi and English — the whole app is in Hindi; you can switch to English.

No account needed to look: prices, weather, schemes and listings can be seen without logging in. Create an account to post your own listing. You can delete your account at any time from the Profile page.

Please note:
• Kissan Sahyog is not a government app and is not affiliated with any government body. Government information is taken from public government sources; check the official source for final details.
• Kissan Sahyog is only a platform for information and contact. No money changes hands in the app. Check the other party yourself before any deal and never send money in advance.
• The app shows no ads.
